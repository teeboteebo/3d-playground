Colours are screen approximations of the hex values, and real paint shifts with your light. Room is 430 × 395 cm with a 330 cm ceiling. The bed follows IKEA TONSTAD 160×200 (169 × 209 cm, headboard 116 cm) and the dresser Mio TOVA (80 × 42 × 100 cm); both are simplified shapes, and the mirror shows a stylised reflection. Wall moulding runs along the bed wall: a rail at sill height (80 cm) with three columns of panels, big above and small below; the bedside tables and lamps sit in the side panels. The ceiling pendant follows IKEA GULLSUDARE 70 cm (rice paper) at the centre of the room, hung with about 2 m clearance below. The foot-of-bed bench follows ILVA Amigo (90 × 35 × 45 cm, solid oak, woven seat; the shelf and seat weave are simplified). Bedside tables follow AC Design Furniture Cholet (50 × 35 × 25 cm, wall-mounted) and the lamps Mio Adina (26 × 18 × 20 cm). Floor, lamp and the window and door positions are assumed. The front wall is hidden so you can look in; use the Dresser wall view to see it. Rug prices and stock were checked on 3 Nov 2026 and change often.
